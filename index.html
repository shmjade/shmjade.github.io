Projects

Research Project

BrainIAC Epilepsy Probing

Confound-Aware Linear Probing Study

Investigated whether BrainIAC, a brain MRI foundation model, linearly encodes epilepsy-relevant concepts from frozen embeddings of 340 T1w/FLAIR scans (Bonn FCD-II dataset). Designed a confound-aware evaluation protocol, controlling for scanner/era effects with a permutation-null analysis, then probed for FCD-II lesions and hemispheric lateralization with subject-level cross-validation.

Python · PyTorch · scikit-learn · MLflow · Foundation Models
